## Discharge of the Elbe River, Gauge Dresden in m³/s ##

elbe-1806.csv : Combined dataset 1806 to 2026, column "provider"
                distinguishes data source and licence (see below)
elbe-2000.csv : Recent data 2000 to 2026 (raw data, Source: WSV / PEGELONLINE)
elbe.csv      : Reduced Teaching Subset 1989 to 2019 (validated data, Source: BfG)

Status        : Dataset updated 2026-10-10

---------------------------------------------------------------------
Data Sources & Terms of Use
---------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT: "elbe-1806.csv" is a COMBINED dataset containing data under
TWO DIFFERENT LICENCES. The column "provider" identifies which licence
applies to each record:

  provider = "BfG"  -> records up to 2019-12-31  (see 1. below)
  provider = "WSV"  -> records from 2020-01-01   (see 2. below)

Data Adaptation:
  Data files adapted and merged by T. Petzoldt for teaching purposes.
  Adaptation comprises unit-consistent merging, rounding of WSV daily
  means to integer m³/s to match BfG precision, and addition of the
  columns "provider" and "validated". No values were otherwise altered.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
Data Quality & Limitations
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Data have been published by the operating waterway and shipping
  authorities.
- Data errors and inconsistencies cannot completely be excluded.
- Neither the WSV, BfG, nor the adapter guarantees the correctness or
  completeness of the data. No guarantee for correctness of the data!
- The combined series is NOT HOMOGENEOUS. Provider, validation status,
  measurement technique and rating curve (W-Q relation) changed over
  the period of record. Comparison of the overlapping years 2020-2021
  showed differences between BfG and WSV values.
  Homogeneity should be verified before trend or extreme-value analysis.
- Data from the 19th century are particularly uncertain as control
  measurements for runoff were not carried out prior to 1900.
- Data starting November 1st, 1889 were revised under the project
  "W-Q Elbe 1890".
- All values are daily mean discharges, averaged from midnight to
  midnight local time (CET/CEST). Recent data are means of 15-minute
  values; historical data were compiled from manual readings or paper
  level recorders.
